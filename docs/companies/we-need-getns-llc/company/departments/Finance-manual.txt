WE NEED GENTS LC | FINANCE AND COMMERCIAL CONTROL MANUAL

Document FIN-MAN-01 | Version 3.2 | APPROVED | effective 2026-09-15
Owner: Owen Patel. All names, systems, events, and requirements are fictional.
This manual includes general company practices beyond any single assigned task. Only applicable clauses govern a specific decision.

FIN-01. Cost center ownership
Each cost center has a named owner, operational purpose, lifecycle status, and posting period. A closed center cannot accept a new commitment. Labels in a presentation do not authorize the creation of a new center in the ledger.

FIN-01.1 Intake and ownership
The Finance and Commercial Control coordinator registers the incoming cost center ownership item, checks the relevant project and effective date, and assigns an owner. The register entry links to the received source rather than replacing it with a paraphrase. If the request combines unrelated items, separate their owners and completion conditions. Reassigning an item does not reset the original receipt date. An incorrect department assignment is returned with a brief explanation and a suggested destination, without inventing a completed decision.

FIN-01.2 Evidence and version handling
For cost center ownership, reviewers distinguish source-system observations, exported snapshots, proposals, approvals, and final records. Keep author, version, and evidence date visible. If two records disagree, record the conflicting values and apply the controlling policy for that process. Do not pick the most convenient value. When the process lacks an explicit precedence rule, mark the issue unresolved and request clarification. A filename containing the word final is not itself proof of approval.

FIN-01.3 Example and exception
Suppose the Brookhaven demonstration project submits a cost center ownership request on Monday and sends a correction on Wednesday. The owner retains the Monday record, links the Wednesday correction, and identifies exactly which fields changed. Unchanged fields remain attributable to the earlier source. If a manager is absent, the coordinator records the pending approval and its operational consequence rather than signing on the manager's behalf. An expedited review changes queue priority; it does not change the acceptance requirements.

FIN-01.4 Completion and handover
A cost center ownership item closes only when the documented deliverable exists, mandatory checks are recorded, and remaining exceptions have a named owner. The handover note includes stable identifiers, file references, and the next action. A downstream reader should be able to verify the outcome without reconstructing a chat conversation. For recurring work, update the relevant work instruction only after review; for a one-time exception, preserve the case note without rewriting company policy.

FIN-01.5 Reporting and retention
The Finance and Commercial Control dashboard distinguishes open, awaiting evidence, awaiting approval, complete, and cancelled items. Count stable item IDs rather than message volume. Report missing evidence separately from confirmed failure. Store the final record with its applicable sources and access controls. Department statistics may be used for capacity planning and lessons learned, but should not become a substitute for the individual case evidence needed to support an operational decision.

FIN-06. Mobilization budgets
The mobilization register expresses amounts in integer US cents. Compare a single worker request to the applicable per-worker limit; do not reinterpret it as a shared pool or apply exchange rates. Active status and numerical limits are separate conditions.

For contractor mobilization, Finance PASS requires an ACTIVE cost center, charge_type exactly MOB-IND, and requested_cents no greater than 120000 for that individual worker. The 120000-cent limit is per worker, not a cumulative batch limit. Explicit failure is HOLD; missing inputs are UNKNOWN.
