WE NEED GENTS LC | SALES AND CLIENT SERVICES MANUAL

Document SAL-MAN-01 | Version 3.2 | APPROVED | effective 2026-09-15
Owner: Sam Cedar. All names, systems, events, and requirements are fictional.
This manual includes general company practices beyond any single assigned task. Only applicable clauses govern a specific decision.

SAL-01. Opportunity qualification
Opportunity qualification records identify a responsible owner, the affected location or deliverable, an effective date, and the source evidence. Sales and Client Services distinguishes a request from an approved action and a completed action from an unverified claim. Use the department register to preserve those distinctions; do not silently change historical entries to match a later report.

SAL-01.1 Intake and ownership
The Sales and Client Services coordinator registers the incoming opportunity qualification item, checks the relevant project and effective date, and assigns an owner. The register entry links to the received source rather than replacing it with a paraphrase. If the request combines unrelated items, separate their owners and completion conditions. Reassigning an item does not reset the original receipt date. An incorrect department assignment is returned with a brief explanation and a suggested destination, without inventing a completed decision.

SAL-01.2 Evidence and version handling
For opportunity qualification, reviewers distinguish source-system observations, exported snapshots, proposals, approvals, and final records. Keep author, version, and evidence date visible. If two records disagree, record the conflicting values and apply the controlling policy for that process. Do not pick the most convenient value. When the process lacks an explicit precedence rule, mark the issue unresolved and request clarification. A filename containing the word final is not itself proof of approval.

SAL-01.3 Example and exception
Suppose the Brookhaven demonstration project submits a opportunity qualification request on Monday and sends a correction on Wednesday. The owner retains the Monday record, links the Wednesday correction, and identifies exactly which fields changed. Unchanged fields remain attributable to the earlier source. If a manager is absent, the coordinator records the pending approval and its operational consequence rather than signing on the manager's behalf. An expedited review changes queue priority; it does not change the acceptance requirements.

SAL-01.4 Completion and handover
A opportunity qualification item closes only when the documented deliverable exists, mandatory checks are recorded, and remaining exceptions have a named owner. The handover note includes stable identifiers, file references, and the next action. A downstream reader should be able to verify the outcome without reconstructing a chat conversation. For recurring work, update the relevant work instruction only after review; for a one-time exception, preserve the case note without rewriting company policy.

SAL-01.5 Reporting and retention
The Sales and Client Services dashboard distinguishes open, awaiting evidence, awaiting approval, complete, and cancelled items. Count stable item IDs rather than message volume. Report missing evidence separately from confirmed failure. Store the final record with its applicable sources and access controls. Department statistics may be used for capacity planning and lessons learned, but should not become a substitute for the individual case evidence needed to support an operational decision.
