WE NEED GENTS LC | TECHNOLOGY SERVICES MANUAL

Document ITS-MAN-01 | Version 3.2 | APPROVED | effective 2026-09-15
Owner: Priya Moss. All names, systems, events, and requirements are fictional.
This manual includes general company practices beyond any single assigned task. Only applicable clauses govern a specific decision.

ITS-01. Service requests
A request includes the affected service, business need, requester identity, and desired date. The ticket owner distinguishes an incident from a planned access change. A task is not completed merely because an automated message was sent.

ITS-01.1 Intake and ownership
The Technology Services coordinator registers the incoming service requests item, checks the relevant project and effective date, and assigns an owner. The register entry links to the received source rather than replacing it with a paraphrase. If the request combines unrelated items, separate their owners and completion conditions. Reassigning an item does not reset the original receipt date. An incorrect department assignment is returned with a brief explanation and a suggested destination, without inventing a completed decision.

ITS-01.2 Evidence and version handling
For service requests, reviewers distinguish source-system observations, exported snapshots, proposals, approvals, and final records. Keep author, version, and evidence date visible. If two records disagree, record the conflicting values and apply the controlling policy for that process. Do not pick the most convenient value. When the process lacks an explicit precedence rule, mark the issue unresolved and request clarification. A filename containing the word final is not itself proof of approval.

ITS-01.3 Example and exception
Suppose the Brookhaven demonstration project submits a service requests request on Monday and sends a correction on Wednesday. The owner retains the Monday record, links the Wednesday correction, and identifies exactly which fields changed. Unchanged fields remain attributable to the earlier source. If a manager is absent, the coordinator records the pending approval and its operational consequence rather than signing on the manager's behalf. An expedited review changes queue priority; it does not change the acceptance requirements.

ITS-01.4 Completion and handover
A service requests item closes only when the documented deliverable exists, mandatory checks are recorded, and remaining exceptions have a named owner. The handover note includes stable identifiers, file references, and the next action. A downstream reader should be able to verify the outcome without reconstructing a chat conversation. For recurring work, update the relevant work instruction only after review; for a one-time exception, preserve the case note without rewriting company policy.

ITS-01.5 Reporting and retention
The Technology Services dashboard distinguishes open, awaiting evidence, awaiting approval, complete, and cancelled items. Count stable item IDs rather than message volume. Report missing evidence separately from confirmed failure. Store the final record with its applicable sources and access controls. Department statistics may be used for capacity planning and lessons learned, but should not become a substitute for the individual case evidence needed to support an operational decision.

ITS-06. Contractor access
Contractor access is a recommendation until the authorized provisioning tool executes it. A report may recommend ALLOW without actually creating an account. Keep recommended access, recorded access, and executed provisioning events distinct in storage and UI.
