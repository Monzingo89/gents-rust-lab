# Public research through an unchanged privacy gate

The first owner-enabled research trial could not retain its official Python page
reads: the native privacy classifier found email-like text. A protected autonomy
source read was also denied. That trial was stopped with no pending effect;
its three tool journals and question remain retained. No report was accepted.

A separate local-model task authored `scripts/public_research.py`. The first
Qwen3-Coder draft failed syntax/import review and was never executed. The
Qwen3.8-27B candidate passed ten independently frozen offline checks, including
nested JSON, validation, copy isolation, Unicode whitespace and long-token work.
Its implementation is copied byte-for-byte from the model's native candidate.
The native candidate itself retains its quarantined submission flags.

The helper creates a conservative, lossy view: whole whitespace-delimited tokens
containing an at-sign are replaced without a reverse mapping. This can remove
code decorators as well as contact text. It is not complete de-identification or
exact executable source. The broker exposes removal counts and loss metadata,
limits the complete serialized view to 96 KB, and sends the result through the
same native privacy gate. No domain exemption or classifier relaxation was added.

Host probes of both official Python pages passed the unchanged Rust classifier.
Residual SSN and payment-card fixtures remained blocked. Thirty broker regression
checks, two native privacy checks, strict workspace lint, full workspace tests
and a fresh build passed. The helper is included in capability-revision identity;
its source, research defaults and the owner policy installer require owner review
through the existing protected-source boundary.

A fresh approved company trial then read both official pages through ordinary
action review. The retained successful tool results reported 11 and 2 removed
tokens. The six-role policy remained at revision 1. Those observed reads qualify
this bounded tool repair; they do not qualify the still-running research report.
The owner-provided observer reference and reviewed autonomy excerpt are inputs,
not model-authored deliverables or permission changes.

Repair campaign wall time: **23.3 minutes**, from the first helper trial through
the second successful governed page read. This includes the rejected draft,
host integration, checks and new-trial planning. Earlier blocked discovery and
later source publication are separate. The successful author trial took 162.51
seconds. Provider/tool fees were **$0**; no model downloads or weight training
occurred. Hardware, electricity, existing services and host development are
separate. The completion email was sent before source/website publication.
