## What the measurements mean

A fixture is qualified only after actual model provenance, owner approval and frozen scope, canonical native receipts, exact current source/test bytes, complete source readback and final independent gates pass. The journal preserves failed planning, failed admission, historical adoption, resumed execution and fresh execution as distinct records. It does not reclassify a runtime-completed false positive as a qualified success.

The sixth fixture declares industry `synthetic_fintech_arithmetic`, modality `text` and a reviewer-assigned `medium` complexity label. These are workload dimensions, not certification or an empirical rating of arbitrary fintech work. Existing fixtures without a reviewed difficulty label stay unclassified. Audio, video, hybrid work and actual banking/healthtech operational competence remain unqualified.

Track completed local replies and their measured input/output tokens, failed/unknown-usage calls, native checks, elapsed time, scripted planning repairs, manual operator answers, resumes and final independent outcome. Do not count historical adoption as new task execution or sum the same planning session again across recovery reports. A reduced reply count on one repeated visible fixture is an observation, not evidence of general intelligence improvement. Report source/build/model/config versions and fixture hashes when comparing fresh trials.

The inspectable curriculum index now shows measured completed replies, local tokens, resumes, operator answers and provider charges alongside admitted attempts. Failed attempts without an admitted aggregate explicitly say so and link to their full saved evidence. Local token usage is not zero; provider/tool charges may be zero while electricity, storage, compute time and frontier repository-development work still have costs.

## Remaining goal

More independent task families, prospective held-out cases and richer modality coverage are needed before broader capability claims. Hardware-aware model acquisition and admission, portable isolated execution, local training/evaluation/promotion, communication/memory backend verification and industry-specific reviewed controls remain active work. No universal task competence, regulatory compliance or absence-of-vulnerabilities claim follows from this curriculum.
