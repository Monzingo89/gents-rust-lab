The easy/medium/hard local research goal is complete within the owner's
explicit clarification on 5 November 2026: **"Synthetic local research only."**

frontier equivalence, blanket compliance or production readiness.

The previous decision was blocked pending this owner scope decision, not a
failed experimental stage. That historical blocker is now resolved. Real
banking deployment, regulated records and external financial effects remain
outside the accepted scope and denied by the synthetic-only profile.

## Accepted scope and exclusions

Owner answer: synthetic local research only. No production release, real
customer/health/card records, transfers or external financial effects are
authorized. Keep the current synthetic-only profile and refusal gates enabled.
The medium result is evaluated prompt/procedure tuning, not weight training;
book use is limited to the supplied public publisher material, not the full
unavailable book. Earlier failures and all documented limitations remain.

A future real banking service would be a separate scope decision requiring
jurisdiction, business function, data categories, deployment target and an
accountable release approver, followed by a reviewed control matrix and new
evidence. It is not an unfinished requirement of this clarified research goal.
The building remains a walking visualization, separate from actual research
execution. No additional inference or continuous monitoring is needed to
complete the accepted finite experiments.
